# Lucento launch playbook

**Goal:** as many people as possible install Lucento from Google Play and keep using it. The budget is **₱0** and the
owner's time is about **40 minutes a week** (owner decisions 2026-11-14: strictly free, faceless content, weekly ready
pack that the owner schedules). Agents do everything else.

Companion files: [`TRUTH_RULES.md`](TRUTH_RULES.md) (what we may say), [`CHANNEL_KIT.md`](CHANNEL_KIT.md) (bios,
hashtags, links, saved replies), [`OWNER_STEPS.md`](OWNER_STEPS.md) (the owner's click-by-click tasks). Production
files (templates, renders, weekly packs) live in `lucento-studio/marketing/` and `lucento-studio/video/src/shorts/`.

## 1. How a new account with no followers reaches people

1. **Short vertical video.** TikTok, Facebook and Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts show a new account's videos to
   strangers first, so followers aren't needed to be seen. One 9:16 video file is posted to all four. Volume and
   consistency matter more than polish: five short videos a week, every week.
2. **Borrowed audiences.** Communities, micro-creators and press already have the people we want. We earn a mention
   with something useful, never by spamming.
3. **Search.** People search Google Play for "budget app" and "expense tracker". Ratings, review replies, the listing
   video and steady installs move Lucento up. Later, website guides can catch Google searches.
4. **Word of mouth.** Shared invites and a "Tell a friend" share carry a tagged Play link, so every happy user is a
   channel.

Free reach comes in spikes: most videos get a few hundred views and a few get thousands. We don't promise numbers. We
measure, keep what works and drop what doesn't.

## 2. Positioning

- **Lead promise:** "Everything about your money, in one clear view." (website, Page and listing)
- **Sign-off:** "See your money clearly." (launch film)
- **Hook that proves it fastest:** typing a whole day in one message. "Type your gastos like a text." (PH)
- **Proof points, in order of how surprising they are:** one message becomes several transactions · a month of
  entries from one statement screenshot · Safe to spend sets bills aside first · "Can I afford it?" answered from your
  own records · one net worth with have above and owe below · no bank login, works offline.

### Shape of every short (8–15 s, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps)

1. **0–1.5 s, hook:** big text plus motion stating the problem or the surprise. No logo first.
2. **1.5–11 s, proof:** a real screen (camera moves over full-resolution stills, typed text animating in the composer),
   one idea only.
3. **Last 2–3 s, sign-off:** the crest, "Lucento", and the CTA line for the phase (section 6).
4. **Safe areas:** keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right 15% (platform buttons and captions sit there).
5. **Sound:** the original synthesized score, or silence with on-screen text.

## 6. Phases

**P-day** is the day Google grants production access and the release is live. The closed test (12+ testers, 14 days in
a row) is running; then the owner applies, and Google usually answers within about a week.

**Dates (2026-11-14):** the closed test runs through Testers Community (15 of 15 testers, a 16-day plan) and started
on Saturday 14 November. The 14 days are done on 28 November and the plan on 30 November, so the owner applies on
**Monday 30 November** and **P-day is expected between 1 and 8 December**. Play Console's Dashboard is the authority on
the day count; if it shows a different number, move these dates with it. December is a good month to land in: the 13th
month pay and Christmas spending give the launch posts their hook.

**No pre-registration (owner, 2026-11-15):** Google Play pre-registration unlocks for this account only after the
testing requirement, so the owner chose to launch as soon as production access is granted instead of holding the
release to collect pre-registrations.

| Phase | When | Call to action in content | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Now → P-day | "Coming soon to Google Play. Follow for launch." (+ the website) | Teach the algorithm who we are, build followers, find what hooks work |
| Launch week | P-day → +7 | "Get it on Google Play" with the tagged link | Every channel at once (run-of-show below) |
| Sustain | Weeks 2–8 | Play link, plus "Join the Lucento Community" | Double down on winning formats, reviews, creators |

### Launch-day run-of-show (outline; the minute-by-minute version comes with the launch kit)

1. Morning: the release is live, and the website PR flips `playListed: true` (owner merges).
2. Launch film posted: the full film pinned on the Facebook Page and on YouTube; the 30 s vertical on TikTok and Reels.
   The film is held until launch day (owner, 2026-11-15). Before then: replace its four-in-one capture, whose message
   names a ride-hailing brand (T21), fix the voice sheet's doubled header, and end it on "Get it on Google Play".
3. Group announcement, plus a personal note to the closed testers the owner recruited personally: thanks, and "tell
   us what you think on Google Play" (no reward, everyone asked, T25). Testers who came through Testers Community are
   thanked but never asked to rate (T25).
4. KlaroPH: launch post on the KlaroPH Page, and the Klaro banner and tools card switch to the Play link. The Klaro
   email starts the next day in batches (section 8, T33).
5. Creator and press pitches go out (personal, one at a time, T28).
6. Days 1–3: owner posts in the communities that allow it, with disclosure (T26). Product Hunt mid-week for the global
   audience. AlternativeTo listing.
7. Every day that week: replies to comments and Play reviews from drafts.

## 7. Borrowed audiences: rules of engagement

- **Communities:** read the rules first; many allow app posts only in a weekly thread or with admin approval, so ask.
  Be useful before posting a link. Disclose (T26). Never cross-post the same text on the same day.
- **Creators:** PH personal-finance and productivity micro-creators (roughly 5k–100k followers; small accounts answer
  and their audiences trust them). Offer: a year of Lucento Pro through a grant, no payment, no script, honest opinions,
  gifted access disclosed (T27). The owner sends each pitch from lucentoapp@gmail.com.
- **Press:** PH tech and lifestyle sites. Angle: a Filipino-made budget app where you type your gastos like a text, no
  bank login. A press kit (fact sheet, logo, real screens, film) in a shared folder.
- **Directories:** Product Hunt (global), AlternativeTo (as an alternative to other budget apps, without naming them in
  our own copy, T17).

## 8. The KlaroPH bridge

KlaroPH (klaroph.com, the owner's web app) keeps running. Its channels introduce Lucento as **the Android app from
the same maker** (owner decisions 2026-11-14: keep both, keep the KlaroPH Page and cross-promote, in-app banner plus
one email). It's the one audience that already knows and trusts the owner, so it should give the first installs.

| Klaro channel | Warm-up (now → P-day) | P-day and after | Who builds it |
|---|---|---|---|
| KlaroPH Facebook Page | 1 post a week: "Something new from the maker of KlaroPH", teaser shorts shared from the Lucento Page | Launch post with the Play link (`utm_source=klaroph`); then share Lucento's best short every week or two | In the weekly pack; scheduled in Meta Business Suite (both Pages live there) |
| Klaro web app, signed-in users | Dismissible banner: "Coming soon: Lucento, our new Android app" → lucentoapp.com | Banner switches to "Get Lucento on Google Play" (Android browsers) or "Lucento is on Android" (others) | A klaroph-app PR (owner merges) |
| klaroph.com tools, calculators and guides | A small "From the maker of KlaroPH" card linking lucentoapp.com | The card points to Google Play | Same PR |
| Klaro users' email | Nothing | **One** announcement email from KlaroPH, starting the day after P-day, in daily batches | Klaro's existing marketing-email path; owner approves the text |

Rules for the bridge (also in `TRUTH_RULES.md`, T30–T33):

- **Same maker, positive framing.** "KlaroPH on the web, Lucento on your phone." Never call Klaro worse in public: it
  still has users. Lucento's own channels don't mention Klaro, except "from the maker of KlaroPH" where it helps trust.
- **Respect Klaro's consent.** The email goes only to users who haven't unsubscribed
  (`profiles.marketing_emails_unsubscribed_at IS NULL`), carries Klaro's unsubscribe link and is sent as KlaroPH. It's
  covered by Klaro's privacy policy ("product updates, feature announcements, and other relevant KlaroPH
  communications").
- **Keep the data apart.** Klaro users' emails never go into Lucento's Supabase, Resend audiences or any Lucento list.
  Lucento's privacy policy doesn't cover them.
- **Protect Lucento's own email on launch day.** Lucento and Klaro share one Resend free tier (100 emails a day). Send
  the Klaro email from P-day + 1, at most 50 a day, so Lucento's sign-up verification emails always have room.
- **Later, not now:** helping Klaro users move their data (a Klaro export that Lucento's Restore accepts) and any
  thank-you for paying Klaro subscribers are owner decisions for after launch.

## 10. Measuring

- **Before P-day:** followers per channel, views per video (median, not best), profile visits, website visits by
  referrer.
- **After P-day:** Play Console installs and store-listing visitors by UTM source (`CHANNEL_KIT.md` links), ratings,
  plus the above.
- **Weekly numbers form (owner, 2 min):** for each channel, followers and the views of each of the week's videos;
  Play installs and listing visitors (after P-day). Website numbers come from Vercel automatically.
- **Decision rules:**
  - A format or hook that beats the channel's median views two weeks running gets two more variations.
  - A format below the median three times is dropped.
  - Never judge a video before 72 hours.
- **What we don't do (₱0):** no ads or boosts, no paid tools, no bought followers, likes or reviews, no engagement
  pods, no mass DMs.
