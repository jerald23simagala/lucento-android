# Owner steps for the Lucento launch

These are the only parts Claude can't do for you: they need your accounts, your phone or your click. Each step says
how long it takes. Apps rename buttons from time to time; if a label doesn't match, pick the closest one, or send a
screenshot and ask.

**Safety first:** never give anyone your passwords or sign-in codes, Claude included. Turn on 2-step verification
on every account you create below.

## Part A: set up once (about 1 to 1½ hours, any time this week)

### A1. Check the closed test and tell Claude the dates (5 min)
1. Open https://play.google.com/console and choose **Lucento**.
2. Open **Dashboard**. Find the box about **production access** ("Apply for production"). It shows how many testers
   have opted in and how many of the 14 days have passed.
3. Write down the day the 14 days started (or how many are left) and send it to Claude. Claude uses it to put
   **P-day** (launch day) on the calendar.
4. Until you apply, **don't remove testers**, and ask them to stay opted in. If someone drops out, the count can fall
   below 12 and the 14 days can start again. Two or three spare testers are cheap insurance: add their emails on the
   closed test's **Testers** tab, then send them the opt-in link shown on that tab.

### A2. Facebook Page and Group (skip if already done)
Follow `docs/community/facebook/README.md`, which has every text and picture. When done, send Claude the Page's
link and the Group's link. (Done: both are live, and the Page's username `lucentoapp` was set on 23 November, so the
Page is https://www.facebook.com/lucentoapp.)

### A3. Instagram (15 min, on your phone)
1. Install **Instagram** and tap **Create new account**. Sign up with **lucentoapp@gmail.com**.
2. Username: `lucentoapp` (fallbacks in `CHANNEL_KIT.md` §1). Name: **Lucento · Budget App**.
3. Add the profile picture `profile-720x720.png`, which is in your **Downloads\lucento-channel-kit** folder (with
   the Facebook covers). Copy it to your phone first, for example by emailing it to yourself.
4. Make it a professional account: **Profile** → **☰** (top right) → **Account type and tools** → **Switch to
   professional account** → **Business** → category **App Page** (or the closest to apps or software) → contact email
   **lucentoapp@gmail.com**.
5. When it asks to connect Facebook, choose the **Lucento** Page. If it doesn't ask: **☰** → **Accounts Center** →
   **Accounts** → **Add accounts** → your Facebook profile, then link the Lucento Page.
6. Paste the bio and link from `CHANNEL_KIT.md` §2 (**Edit profile**).
7. Turn on 2-step verification: **☰** → **Accounts Center** → **Password and security** → **Two-factor
   authentication**.

### A4. TikTok (15 min, on your phone)
1. Install **TikTok**, tap **Sign up** → **Use phone or email** → **Email** → **lucentoapp@gmail.com**. Use your
   real birthday (it must show an adult).
2. Username `lucentoapp`. Name **Lucento · Budget App**. Add the same profile picture.
3. Stay on a personal account. If TikTok offers a Business account and then asks you to **Verify your business**
   (company certificate, license number), tap back: Lucento doesn't need it, because we don't pay for promotion.
4. Paste the bio from `CHANNEL_KIT.md` §2 (it includes lucentoapp.com as text; the link becomes clickable at 1,000
   followers).
5. Turn on 2-step verification: **Settings and privacy** → **Security** → **2-step verification**.

### A5. YouTube (10 min, on a computer)
1. Go to https://www.youtube.com and sign in with **lucentoapp@gmail.com**.
2. Click the profile picture (top right) → **Create a channel**. Name **Lucento**, handle `@lucentoapp`, then
   **Create channel**.
3. **Customize channel** → **Profile**: upload the picture, paste the description from `CHANNEL_KIT.md` §2, and add a
   link (title "Lucento", the YouTube link from §3). Click **Publish**.
4. Banner (added 15 Nov): **Customize channel** → **Profile** → **Banner image** → **Upload** →
   `Downloads\lucento-channel-kit\youtube-banner-2560x1440.png` → **Done** → **Publish**.

### A6. Meta Business Suite (5 min, on a computer)
1. Go to https://business.facebook.com. In the top-left switcher, check that you see the **Lucento** Page (with
   Instagram) and the **KlaroPH** Page.
2. If Instagram is missing: **Settings** → **Accounts** → **Instagram accounts** → **Connect**.
   This is where you'll schedule Facebook and Instagram posts every week.

### A7. Send Claude a short note
- Which handles you got (or which fallback).
- The links to the Lucento Page, the Group, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
- The closed-test dates from A1.
- From the KlaroPH founder dashboard: roughly how many Klaro users can get marketing email (not unsubscribed). This
  sets how many days the Klaro email will take at 50 a day.

## Part B: when the 14 days are done (30 min)

1. Tell Claude first: **"Draft my production access answers."** Claude writes them from your testers' real feedback.
2. Play Console → **Dashboard** → **Apply for production**. Paste the answers, check them, then submit.
3. Google usually answers within about a week. When it says yes, tell Claude: that sets the launch day in motion.

## Part C: every week (about 40 minutes)

**Monday or Tuesday: approve (5–10 min, on your phone).** Open the **Lucento Launch Board** (Claude sends the link).
For each card, watch the video or look at the picture, read the caption, and tap **Approve** or **Request change**
(say what to change).

**Tuesday: schedule (20–30 min, on a computer).** Open this week's pack folder,
`Downloads\lucento-ready-packs\<week>\`. `SCHEDULE.md` lists every post with its file, caption, date and time. For
each short:

1. **TikTok:** https://www.tiktok.com/tiktokstudio → **Upload** → choose the video → paste the caption → if it shows
   **Disclose post content**, turn it on and choose **Your brand** → if there's a **Schedule** option, set the date and
   time → **Schedule** (up to 10 days ahead). If there's no Schedule option on your personal account, post each video
   from the TikTok app on your phone at its time instead (you can save it as a draft beforehand).
2. **Facebook and Instagram Reels together:** https://business.facebook.com → **Lucento** → **Create reel** → upload
   the same video → tick both the **Facebook Page** and **Instagram** → paste the caption → **Schedule** (date and
   time) → **Schedule**.
3. **YouTube Shorts:** https://studio.youtube.com → **Create** → **Upload videos** → the same video → title and
   description from the pack → **No, it's not made for kids** → **Next** until **Visibility** → **Schedule** → date and
   time → **Schedule**.

For Page posts (pictures): Meta Business Suite → **Create post** → choose **Lucento** (or **KlaroPH** for the Klaro
post) → add the pictures → paste the text → **Schedule**.

**Sunday: numbers (2 min).** On the Launch Board's **Numbers** tab, type each channel's followers and each video's
views (each app's profile and its insights or analytics page shows them). After launch, add Play Console's installs
and store listing visitors (**Statistics**).

**Any day (optional, 5–10 min).** Reply to comments with the saved replies in `CHANNEL_KIT.md` §7. Answering early
comments helps a video travel further.

## Part D: launch day (about 2 hours)

Claude sends a minute-by-minute checklist before launch day. In short: publish the release, merge the website PR,
post the launch film, post in the Group and on KlaroPH, thank your testers, send the creator and press emails, and
reply to everyone.
