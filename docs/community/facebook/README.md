# Lucento on Facebook: community kit

A Facebook **Page** called "Lucento" (the brand's home) runs a Facebook **Group** called "Lucento Community", where
people suggest ideas, ask questions and report problems. This folder holds every graphic and every piece of text
needed to set both up and run the first month. Made 2026-11-09.

## Setting it up (step by step)

Facebook renames buttons now and then. If a label below doesn't match, look for the nearest equivalent.

**1. Create the Page** (on a computer, at facebook.com, signed in to your own account)
1. Click **Menu** (the grid icon at top right), then **Page**. (On some accounts it's **Pages › Create new Page**.)
2. Page name: `Lucento`. Category: type `App Page` and pick it (or `Software`).
3. Bio: paste the **Page bio** below. Click **Create Page**.
4. Profile picture: upload `profile-720x720.png`. Cover photo: upload `page-cover-1640x624.png`, and don't drag it to reposition.
5. Under **Contact**, add the website `https://www.lucentoapp.com` and the email `lucentoapp@gmail.com`.
6. Later, in **Settings › Page setup › Username**, try `lucentoapp` (Facebook only accepts it if nobody else has it).

**2. Create the Group as the Page** (so admin posts show as "Lucento", not your personal name)
1. Switch to the Page: click your profile photo (top right), then **Lucento**.
2. **Menu › Group**. Name: `Lucento Community`. Privacy: see the choice below. Visibility: **Visible**.
3. Upload `group-cover-1640x856.png` as the cover.
4. **Description:** paste the **Group description** below.
5. **Rules** (Manage › Group rules): add the five **Group rules** below, one at a time (title + details).
6. **Membership questions** (Manage › Membership questions): add the three below.
7. **Topics** (in the Group settings, if Facebook offers them): `Ideas`, `Questions`, `Problems`, `What's new`, `This week`.
8. Post, in this order, so the newest ends up on top: Problems, Ideas, Rules, Welcome. Pin each one: **⋯ › Mark as announcement** or **Feature**.
9. On the Page, post the **Page announcement** below so Page followers can find the Group.

**Privacy: Private + Visible** (owner decision, 2026-11-09). Anyone can find the group and ask to join, but only
members see the posts. Members will post screenshots of a money app, so a private group protects them better and
matches Lucento's privacy-first promise. Facebook lets a group go from Private to Public later, but never back.

### Group rules (title, then details)
1. **Be kind.** Critique ideas, never people. Disrespect, harassment and hate speech get removed.
2. **Keep your money private.** Hide amounts before you share a screenshot (tap Hide on Home). Never post account
   numbers, card numbers, passwords, sign-in codes or backup files, and never ask anyone else for them. Lucento will
   never ask for them in comments or messages.
3. **Search before you post.** If someone already shared your idea or problem, add your voice there. It helps us
   see what matters most.
4. **No selling or spam.** No ads, referral or affiliate links, investment pitches, loan offers or paid "advice".
5. **Experiences, not advice.** Members share what works for them. Nothing here is financial, tax or legal advice.

### Membership questions
1. Do you use Lucento? *(Yes, every day / Sometimes / Not yet, I'm curious)*
2. What do you most want a money app to help you with?
3. Do you agree to the group rules, including keeping account numbers, passwords and codes out of the group? *(Yes)*

Don't ask for an email address or phone number. Lucento's privacy policy doesn't cover collecting them here.

### Saved replies for admins
Saved replies keep answers consistent. They live in Messenger or Meta Business Suite; in group comments, paste them.

- **Thanks, noted:** "Thanks for this! We've added it to our list. Others who want it too: like the post, it helps us decide what's next."
- **Already asked:** "Thanks! This one's been asked before, here: [link]. Add your voice there so it all counts together. 🙏"
- **Need details:** "Sorry about that! Could you add your phone model, Android version and Lucento version (bottom of the profile menu), and the steps that led to it?"
- **Fixed:** "Good news: this is fixed in Lucento [version]. Update from Google Play and let us know if you still see it."
- **Private matter:** "Let's take this one private so your details stay safe. Please email lucentoapp@gmail.com and include the email you sign in with."
- **Removed for privacy:** "We hid your post because it showed [account details / amounts]. Feel free to post again with Hide on. Thanks for understanding!"

## Running it

- **Answer every post within a day or two**, even if the answer is only "noted". Early members decide whether the
  group feels alive.
- **Never promise dates.** Say "on our list" or "we're working on it", and "it's out" only once it's on Google Play.
- **Close the loop.** When something a member asked for ships, post under the **What's new** topic and tag them.
- **Moderate rule 2 hard.** Hide any post or comment that shows account numbers, card numbers, codes or a backup file,
  then use the saved reply above.
- **Brand truth:** don't promise "no ads" or "free forever", don't name banks or e-wallets as partners, and don't
  post real people's amounts.

## Making new graphics

The source is `source/assets.html`: one `<section class="asset">` per image, using the Horizon tokens. To re-render
(Microsoft Edge must be installed, and Playwright comes from the studio's `node_modules`):

```sh
cd docs/community/facebook/source
NODE_PATH="../../../../../lucento-studio/node_modules" node render.cjs            # all
NODE_PATH="../../../../../lucento-studio/node_modules" node render.cjs post-idea  # one, by section id
```

The PNGs are written to this folder as `<id>-<width>x<height>.png`. The files in `source/assets/` are copies of
`lucento-website/brand/logo` (Rising Crest), `lucento-website/public/fonts/app` (Plus Jakarta Sans, OFL — see
`OFL.txt`) and the app's `drawable-nodpi` mascot and panoramas. Don't edit them; replace them from their source.
