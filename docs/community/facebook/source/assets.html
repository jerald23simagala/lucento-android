House rules

Kind, useful,
and private.

1

Be kind

Critique ideas, never people.

2

Keep your money private

Hide amounts in screenshots. No account numbers, passwords or codes.

3

Search before you post

Add your voice to an existing thread.

4

No selling or spam

No ads, referral links or paid “advice”.

5

Experiences, not advice

Members share what works for them. It isn’t financial advice.
