# Bundled institution assets

Generated by `tools/institutions/build_institutions.py`; regenerate rather than editing. Sources: the bank asset
library (`registry/institutions.json`) and the wallet design system (`card-designs.json`, `institution-icons.json`).
Registry digest: `ad2e2fbfb5a9996b`.

- Institutions bundled: **380** (card networks are not bundled: a wallet is never a network).
- Card logos: **305** vector; the rest use a monogram on the card.
- List icons: **70** vector, **304** official app icons (WebP), **6** monograms.

## Decision: official app icon over a recoloured glyph
Where the design system's order picked `icon.svg` but that icon is a recoloured community glyph (Simple Icons) and
the institution's official app icon exists, the app icon is used. It is the institution's own, recognisable
artwork; the glyph is not. Overrides still win (DANA keeps its icon.svg: its App Store icon is a seasonal promo).
Applies to 44: `br_picpay`, `cn_alipay`, `cn_wechat_pay`, `de_commerzbank`, `de_hypovereinsbank`, `de_n26`, `de_payback`, `de_sparkasse`, `eg_fawry`, `es_caixabank`, `es_sabadell`, `fr_boursobank`, `fr_credit_mutuel`, `fr_lydia`, `gb_chase_uk`, `gb_revolut`, `gb_standard_chartered`, `gb_starling`, `id_jago`, `ie_revolut_ie`, `in_axis`, `in_fampay`, `in_hdfc`, `in_icici`, `in_phonepe`, `in_razorpay`, `jp_rakuten_bank`, `kr_samsung_pay`, `kw_gulf_bank`, `lt_viva_wallet`, `nz_anz_nz`, `ph_grabpay_ph`, `pl_mbank`, `se_klarna`, `sg_grabpay`, `th_scb`, `us_afterpay`, `us_apple_pay`, `us_chase`, `us_payoneer`, `us_paypal`, `us_robinhood`, `us_square`, `us_western_union`.
