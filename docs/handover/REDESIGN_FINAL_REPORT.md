## QA review (read-only `lucento-qa`, 2026-11-03)

There was no blocker. The QA agent's High finding (old clients deleting scoped rows) depends on a fold that exists only in uncommitted pre-redesign builds. `main`'s v9 builds have no such fold, but they still mis-show and can overwrite those rows, so it is recorded as RD-C2 and documented by owner decision. The following were fixed with tests:
- Scope-blind category fold.
- An unreadable institution id hiding the wallet.
- Backup version not raised.
- TalkBack sign on institution cards.
- A removed month described as "has its own amount".
- A guide edit hidden by a month amount.
- `setMonthAmount` atomicity.
- Single-scope check on pull.
- Rotation losing the amount.
- Milestone copy.
- The trend counting new wallets.

The doc inaccuracies were also corrected: Room v10, CALCULATIONS "version 3", the migration KDoc, and WalletCard's "no chip".
