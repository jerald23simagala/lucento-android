## 0. Summary and the decisions you are asked to make

Lucento has **no billing, entitlement or "Pro" code today** — not a flag, not a dependency, not a string. The only
monetization-shaped things in the codebase are three hooks that were deliberately left for this work:

| Hook | Where | What it already does |
|---|---|---|
| `lucento_private.max_partners(owner)` | `supabase/migrations/20261105120000_lucento_shared.sql` | Hardcoded `1`; the one place the Partner limit lives. PRODUCT_SPEC §7 sketches Free 0 / Pro 1 / Pro Plus 2 / Family 4 |
| `public.consume_ai_quota()` + `AI_DAILY_LIMIT` | `20261027090000_ai_usage_quota.sql`, `lucento-website/src/pages/api/{ask,interpret}.ts` | A per-user, per-UTC-day counter; one global limit (50) for everyone |
| `Feature` / `FeatureSet` | `domain/Features.kt` | User *preference* toggles for modules (hide, never delete). Not an entitlement model, but the right shape to sit beside one |

**Recommended product shape** (details in §3–§5):

- **Free stays a complete offline budgeting app**: unlimited wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, transfers,
  debts & loans, scheduled payments, Insights, on-phone Ask Lucento reports, receipt and statement scanning, CSV
  export, JSON backup/restore, app lock, two themes, three active savings goals and a small monthly AI allowance.
- **Lucento Pro** = the things that either cost Lucento money per user or are clearly "more": cloud sync and
  multi-device restore, Lucento Shared, the AI adviser allowance and Smarter reading, wallets in other currencies,
  statements as PDF, all five themes, unlimited savings goals — plus everything built later in those areas
  (budget forecasting, month-end FX revaluation, reminders).
- **Pricing**: one subscription (`lucento_pro`) with **monthly and annual** base plans and a 7-day free trial on
  annual, at launch. **Lifetime is deferred**, not rejected (§4). Prices are never typed into the app; they come
  from Play's `ProductDetails`.

## 1. Phase 1 — Repository audit

### 1.1 Architecture as found

- **App**: single Gradle module, manual DI in `AppContainer` (`LucentoApplication.kt`), ViewModels via
  `core/ViewModelFactory.kt` (`containerFactory`). Pure domain in `domain/` (JVM-tested, 1,088 tests on `main`).
  Room v13 (`exportSchema`, schemas 1–13 committed), DataStore for preferences, Navigation Compose type-safe routes
  (`navigation/LucentoRoot.kt`). Compose-side cross-cutting state already uses CompositionLocals (`feature/fx/FxLocals.kt`,
  `feature/sharedspace/SharedSpaceLocals.kt`) — the natural pattern for an entitlement local.
- **Accounts**: Supabase Auth (Google via Credential Manager, or email + password) in `data/sync/AuthRepository.kt`.
  A device holds one ledger bound to one account (`SyncPreferences.syncedUserId`, `AccountBoundary`,
  `LocalLedgerReset`). The Supabase user id is the only server-side identity Lucento has.
- **Network use** (all opt-in, documented): sync (Supabase), the AI proxy on `lucentoapp.com` (Gemini, `ProxyCall.kt`),
  exchange rates (jsDelivr currency-api → Frankfurter, on by default since multicurrency), Google profile picture on
  request, Crashlytics only with `google-services.json`. There is **no Google Play Services dependency** beyond
  Credential Manager; **no Play Billing Library**.
- **Server**: Supabase Postgres with RLS on every user-owned table, security-definer RPCs with explicit grants,
  append-only migrations (16 on `main`), no Edge Functions. Server logic that needs a secret (the Gemini key) lives
  on **Vercel** in the Astro website (`src/pages/api/ask.ts`, `interpret.ts`, `src/server/gemini.ts`) and
  authenticates callers with their Supabase JWT. This is the proven home for anything that must hold a server
  secret — including a Play Developer API service account.
- **Preferences**: `UserPreferencesRepository` (DataStore `user_preferences`), `SyncPreferences` (DataStore
  `sync_preferences`), small SharedPreferences stores for crash flag, revokes and wipe marker. `resetForNextAccount()`
  and `LocalLedgerReset` define what an account switch clears — an entitlement cache must decide explicitly whether it
  survives that (it should: Pro belongs to the Google Play account, not the sync account — §6.4).
- **Build types**: debug uses `com.lucento.app.debug` and the `lucento-test` Supabase project, never production.
  Play Billing only works for the applicationId published on Play, so debug/CI builds need a fake purchase source
  (§6.6). Release signing is not yet configured (KNOWN_ISSUES CS-20) — a prerequisite for any Play track.
- **Website**: Astro, static; the FAQ says *"A date and pricing haven't been announced."* (`src/data/home.ts:181`).
  No pricing page, no feature matrix beyond the ten marketing cards in `src/data/features.ts`.

## 2. Phase 2 — Monetization decisions

### 2.1 Principles that drove every call below

1. **Free must be a complete budgeting app, offline, forever.** Anything the onboarding presents as a *focus*
   (spending, budgets, savings, debts, recurring, trends) works in Free, at most with a generous count limit.
2. **Pro is paid for by what costs Lucento money or clearly goes beyond one person on one phone**: servers (sync,
   Shared), AI (Gemini), and depth features (multicurrency, PDF statements, extra themes, unlimited goals).
3. **Never paywall safety, privacy or portability**: backup/restore, CSV, app lock, hide amounts, history.
4. **Limits restrict creation, never existence** (§7).
5. **Enforce on the server whatever the server pays for** (AI quota, Partner capacity, sync writes). On the phone,
   enforce in repositories, surface in UI; UI hiding is never the boundary (the Lucento Shared PERMISSION_MATRIX rule).
6. **No prices in code.** Play's `ProductDetails` is the only source of a price string in the app.

### 2.2 Why not cap wallets, categories or scheduled payments (§3.1)

The typical Philippine user already needs cash, GCash, Maya, one or two banks and a credit card: five or six
wallets on day one. A cap of 3–5 would hit normal users immediately and read as a bait-and-switch against the
"409 institutions" promise; a cap of 10+ converts nobody. Categories are the budget; scheduled payments are an
onboarding focus. All three stay unlimited.

### 3.1 Wallets — unlimited (see §2.2).

### 3.2 Multicurrency — Pro (D-6)

The gate is **creating** a wallet in a currency other than the main one (`WalletRepository.create`) and
**changing** a wallet's currency to a foreign one (already funnelled through `WalletChangePolicy`). Everything else —
quoting a row in a foreign wallet, cross-currency transfers to an *existing* foreign wallet, FX display, Insights FX
results — keeps working whatever the plan, so a user who downgrades loses no function on data they already have.
The currency picker in New wallet stays visible; choosing a foreign currency opens the Locked sheet (§5.1) with the
main-currency choice still one tap away.

### 3.3 Savings goals — Free 3 active (D-2)

Why 3 rather than 2: Lucento's own onboarding and guide suggest an emergency fund *and* a first goal; a third
leaves room for the common "trip + gadget + emergency fund" without feeling trapped. Counting **active** goals only
means archiving a reached goal frees a slot — the existing Active / Archived toggle becomes the natural release
valve. Enforced in `GoalRepository` (create and un-archive). A Free user with 5 goals (after a downgrade or a restore)
keeps all 5, can contribute, edit, reach and archive them; only *new* goals wait until they are under 3 or upgrade.

### 3.4 Cloud sync — Pro, sign-in Free (D-1)

Sync is the only feature with a per-user server bill that grows forever, and the strongest known conversion
driver in personal-finance apps. Keeping **sign-in** free matters for three reasons: the free AI allowance needs an
identity for the server-side counter; a Partner must sign in to accept a Shared invitation (the Partner needs no
plan); and a Free user who later upgrades should not have to create an account at that moment.

What a Free (or lapsed) signed-in user gets: an account, the free AI allowance, Shared invitations addressed to
them, and the ability to join a Shared Space. What they do not get: `SyncPreferences.setEnabled(true)`. The sync chip
(Home) shows "Cloud sync is part of Lucento Pro" instead of "Not synced".

Lapse behaviour (§7.2): uploads and pulls **pause**; Room keeps everything; dirty rows wait in the existing queue;
re-subscribing resumes. Server data is never deleted on lapse.

Onboarding: the "I already have Lucento" path (restore from account) is a Pro feature by construction, but the
*wording* must not sell at step one: a lapsed user restoring on a new phone is offered the trial / plans *after*
sign-in, with "Restore a backup file" as the free alternative on the same screen (it already exists on Welcome).

### 3.5 AI — Free taste, Pro allowance (D-3, D-4)

Cost reality: an adviser request sends up to 400 figures plus 60 days of transactions; on a Flash-class model that
is roughly US$0.003–0.01 per answer. 50 per day for everyone is not sustainable for a free tier at any scale; 10 per
month per free user is (worst case ≈ US$0.10 / user / month, realistically far less). The proxy already returns the
remaining count, so the app's copy ("3 of 10 free questions left this month") is a display, not a client-side rule.
Smarter reading has a free equivalent (the on-phone reader) and is Pro outright.

Both limits become **plan-aware on the server**: `consume_ai_quota()` keeps counting per day; the proxy reads the
caller's plan from `public.entitlements` and applies `AI_FREE_MONTHLY_LIMIT` (sum of the month's rows) or
`AI_PRO_DAILY_LIMIT`. The app never decides.

### 3.6 Statement scanning — Free for now (D-7)

It is a genuine differentiator and costs nothing. Flagged as a later Pro candidate once there is conversion data;
the gate would be the classifier's "statement with N rows" result, with receipts always free.

## 4. Pricing model recommendation (D-8)

### 4.1 Plans at launch

| Product | Type | Base plans / offers | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lucento_pro` | Subscription | `monthly` (P1M), `annual` (P1Y) with a **7-day free trial** offer on annual (once per user) | **Launch** |
| `lucento_pro_lifetime` | One-time in-app product (non-consumable) | — | **Defer** (see 4.3) |

Monthly exists so the price of entry is low and PH cash payments (Play's pending transactions via convenience
stores) are practical; annual carries the business. A trial only on annual is the common, honest pattern: it
nudges to the plan that keeps users without a dark pattern on the cheap one. Play lets trial eligibility be limited
to once per subscriber per app, which is what we want.

### 4.2 Indicative prices (for approval; never hardcoded)

Prices are set in Play Console per country from a USD template, then hand-tuned for the Philippines, the primary
market. Lucento's install base is PH-first (36 of 409 bundled institutions are Philippine, the mascot's examples are
in pesos), so PH pricing should be deliberate rather than a straight conversion.

| Market | Monthly | Annual | Annual saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| USD template | $3.99 | $29.99 | ~37% |
| Philippines | ₱149 | ₱1,190 | ~33% |

Rationale: ₱149/month sits at the price of one streaming tier and below a cup of specialty coffee; ₱1,190/year is
under ₱100/month and reads as a deliberate round "about a thousand". Comparable PH-aimed trackers either are free
with ads or price ₱150–₱300/month. Changing these is a Play Console action; the app shows whatever Play returns.

### 4.3 Lifetime — defer, don't refuse

Sync and AI are *recurring* costs; a lifetime price has to be high enough (typically 3–4× annual, ≈ ₱3,500+) to
cover them, which at launch, with no churn data, is a guess that cannot be undone (lifetime buyers must be honoured
forever). Also, a lifetime SKU at launch cannibalises annual while the value story is still forming. Recommend
revisiting after two quarters of data, possibly as a time-boxed "founder" offer. The architecture supports it from
day one (`source = LIFETIME`, no expiry — §6.1), so deferring costs nothing.

### 4.4 Promotions and future tiers

Play promo codes and Play's own introductory-pricing offers need no app change. Server-granted entitlements
(`source = GRANT`, with an expiry) cover founders, testers, giveaways and support goodwill without Play. A future
"Pro Plus" / "Family" (PRODUCT_SPEC §7) is a new `Plan` value plus a `max_partners` change; nothing in the UI matrix
is hardcoded to two plans.

### 4.5 Play policy facts that shape the design

- Google's deprecation schedule required **Play Billing Library 8 or later for all new submissions and updates
  from 31 August 2026** (with an extension available to 1 November 2026); **version 9 shipped in May 2026** and is the
  current major. Lucento starts on 9.x. Sources: [Android Developers — Deprecations](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/play-developer-apis-deprecations),
  [RevenueCat — Play Billing 9.0](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/engineering/play-billing-v9),
  [RevenueCat — migrating v7 → v8](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/engineering/play-billing-8-migration),
  [Codemicros — the 31 Aug 2026 requirement](https://www.codemicros.com/2026/08/google-play-billing-library-8-requirement-august-2026.html).
  One source also ties the deadline to `targetSdk 36` ([Vioside](https://vioside.com/blog/google-plays-august-31-2026-deadline)); Lucento targets 35 today — **verify in Play Console before the first upload**.
- Digital goods inside the app must be sold through Play Billing; the website may describe prices but must not sell
  or deep-link to an alternative checkout.
- Free trials and introductory prices are configured as Play **offers** on a base plan, with eligibility controls
  (once per subscriber per app by default) — [Play Console: Subscription setup](https://play.google.com/console/about/subscriptionsetup/),
  [Brickit — testing trials and intro prices](https://medium.com/brickit-engineering/test-strategy-for-free-trials-and-introductory-prices-in-google-play-8e0e6a0fdf41).
- Trial and renewal terms must be shown plainly before purchase; a "Manage subscription" link
  (`https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions?sku=…&package=…`) and the ability to restore belong on the
  upgrade screen — [AppsOps — subscription disclosure checklist](https://appsops.store/news/google-play-subscription-disclosure-rules-q3-2026) (secondary source; verify against the current Developer Content Policy).
- Purchases must be **acknowledged within three days** or Play refunds them; pending purchases must be enabled and
  handled.

Tone: Lucento explains, it never nags. No launch interstitials, no countdown timers, no "limited time" copy that
isn't true, no red badges, no pre-checked boxes. The mascot (`LucentoMascot`) appears once, on the upgrade screen's
hero, in its confident pose. All copy below is draft.

### 5.1 Locked feature (Pro-only)

A bottom sheet (`ProLockedSheet`), opened from the exact tap that would have used the feature:

[Pro]  Cloud sync is part of Lucento Pro
Keep this phone and your other devices on the same ledger, and restore
everything from your account on a new phone.
Your data stays on this phone whatever you choose. You can always export a backup file.

Rules: one sentence on what it does, one on the benefit, one honest line about the free alternative where one

### 5.2 Usage limit reached

[Pro]  You have 3 active savings goals — the limit on the free plan

### 5.3 Preview access

- **Themes**: tapping Grove / Mono / Aurora applies it live while the picker is open (the existing 250 ms cross-fade),
  with a small "Pro — previewing" chip; closing the picker reverts. Nothing private is exposed.
- **AI adviser**: the free monthly allowance *is* the preview.
- **Lucento Shared**: the Settings page shows the full explainer and the invitation flow up to "Invite", which opens the
  locked sheet. A Partner can always accept (their Owner holds the plan).
- **Multicurrency**: the New wallet currency picker lists every currency; foreign ones carry a Pro badge.
- **PDF statements**: the report is fully visible on screen; only Save as PDF / Share are badged.

### 5.4 Pro badge

`ProBadge()` — a small outlined chip reading "Pro" in the theme's brand accent (`ThemeSpec` tokens, never a
hardcoded colour), 20 dp tall, with `contentDescription = "Lucento Pro feature"`. Used inline next to labels, never
as a lock icon over content. When the user *has* Pro, badges disappear everywhere.

### 5.5 Upgrade screen (`feature/pro/ProScreen`, route `ProRoute(from: String)`)

Sections, top to bottom:

1. **Hero**: themed landscape strip, the mascot, "Lucento Pro", one line: *"Everything in Lucento, on every device —

2. **What you get** — six rows with icons: Cloud sync & restore · Lucento Shared · AI adviser (50 a day) and Smarter

3. **Plan selector** — two cards, Annual (pre-selected, "7-day free trial", per-month equivalent) and Monthly, every

## 6. Technical implementation architecture

### 6.1 Entitlement model (pure Kotlin, `domain/entitlement/`)

```
Plan              FREE | PRO           (extensible: PRO_PLUS, FAMILY …)
EntitlementSource NONE | PLAY_SUBSCRIPTION | PLAY_LIFETIME | GRANT
EntitlementStatus FREE | ACTIVE | TRIAL | GRACE | ON_HOLD | PAUSED | EXPIRED | PENDING | UNVERIFIED | BILLING_UNAVAILABLE

data class EntitlementState(
    plan: Plan, status, source,
    expiresAtMillis: Long?, verifiedAtMillis: Long?, productId: String?, basePlanId: String?,
    autoRenewing: Boolean,
)   // the single observable truth; FREE_DEFAULT when nothing is known

enum class ProFeature { CLOUD_SYNC, SHARED_SPACE, AI_ADVISER, SMART_READING, FOREIGN_CURRENCY_WALLETS,
                        PDF_STATEMENTS, EXTRA_THEMES, SAVINGS_GOALS }

object PlanCatalog {           // data, not logic; one place to change when pricing changes
    fun limit(feature, plan): Limit   // Limit.Unlimited | Limit.None | Limit.Count(n) | Limit.Monthly(n) | Limit.Daily(n)
    fun rows(): List<ComparisonRow>  // feeds the Free vs Pro table
}

object EntitlementEvaluator {  // pure: (state, feature, usage, now) -> Access
    sealed interface Access { Allowed; Locked(feature); LimitReached(used, limit, proLimit) }
}
```

- `EntitlementManager` (in `AppContainer`) owns `val state: StateFlow<EntitlementState>` and
  `fun access(feature, usage: Flow<Int>): Flow<Access>`. It combines the Play source, the server snapshot and the
  cached snapshot (§6.3), recomputes on app start, on `PurchasesUpdatedListener`, on sign-in, and every 12 h while
  open.
- **Where checks live** (and nowhere else):

| Gate | Enforced in | Surfaced by |
|---|---|---|
| Enable cloud sync; background/foreground sync allowed | `SettingsViewModel` → `backgroundSyncAllowed(enabled, onboarded, entitled)` (pure, tested) | Settings › Account, sync chip |
| Shared invite / accept | **Server** `max_partners(owner)` reads `entitlements`; app mirrors from the space summary it already fetches | Shared settings |
| Foreign-currency wallet create / currency change | `WalletRepository.create`, `WalletChangePolicy` → `SaveResult.ProRequired` | Wallet editor |
| Goal create / un-archive | `GoalRepository` → `SaveResult.LimitReached(used, limit)` | Goals |
| PDF save / share | `AskLucentoViewModel` | Report sheet |
| Extra themes | `AppViewModel.setTheme` (preview allowed, persist refused) | Theme picker |
| AI adviser allowance, Smarter reading | **Server** (proxy reads plan); client shows remaining | Ask Lucento, consent dialogs |

  Composables read `LocalEntitlements.current` (a CompositionLocal like `FxLocals`) only to *display* badges and
  copy; they never decide access. This satisfies "no checks scattered across composables" while keeping repositories
  the boundary, exactly as `StructureAccess` does for Lucento Shared.
- `Feature` (module toggles) stays a preference. A module can be *on* and *locked* (Shared), which the UI renders as
  badge + locked sheet, not as a disabled switch.

### 6.2 Billing (`data/billing/`)

```
interface PurchaseSource {                 // Play in release; Fake in debug, CI and JVM tests
    val updates: Flow<PurchaseSnapshot>    // from PurchasesUpdatedListener + queryPurchasesAsync
    suspend fun products(): ProductCatalog // ProductDetails → immutable, UI-safe copies (price strings included)
    suspend fun launch(activity, product, offerToken): LaunchResult
    suspend fun acknowledge(token)
    suspend fun restore(): PurchaseSnapshot
    val availability: StateFlow<BillingAvailability>   // OK | UNAVAILABLE | DISCONNECTED
}
class PlayPurchaseSource(...) : PurchaseSource   // BillingClient 9.x: enablePendingPurchases, auto-reconnection,
                                                 // ProductDetailsParams, PurchasesResult; signature verification of
                                                 // each Purchase with the app's Play public key (local defence)
```

Dependency: `com.android.billingclient:billing-ktx` **9.x** (one library; Apache-2.0; no transitive Play Services
beyond what Credential Manager already brings). Add to `libs.versions.toml` deliberately; verify compileSdk 35 is
accepted by the chosen 9.x release notes (v9 targets API 35).

### 6.3 Entitlement persistence — why it is not a boolean

`EntitlementStore` (DataStore `entitlement_preferences`) persists the **last verified `EntitlementState`** — plan,
status, source, `expiresAt`, `verifiedAt`, a hash of the purchase token, `productId`. Rules:

1. Play is re-queried on every process start (`queryPurchasesAsync` works offline from Play's cache); the stored
   snapshot is only what the UI shows during the first 100 ms and when Play is unreachable.
2. A snapshot grants Pro only while `now < expiresAt + GRACE` (recommend 3 days; Play's own grace/hold are
   reflected in `expiresAt` once verified). A lifetime snapshot needs re-verification against Play at least every
   30 days or it degrades to `UNVERIFIED` (still honoured for a further 7 days, then Free, with a visible notice).
3. Server verification (§6.4) **overrides** the local reading whenever it is newer; a server `REVOKED`/`EXPIRED` wins
   over a stale local snapshot.
4. The store is **not** cleared by `resetForNextAccount()` / `LocalLedgerReset` (Pro belongs to the Google Play
   account on the phone, not to the Lucento sync account), **is** excluded from JSON backups, and is never synced.

### 6.4 Server side (Supabase + Vercel) (D-10)

- New migration `public.entitlements`:
  `user_id uuid pk references auth.users`, `plan text`, `status text`, `source text`, `product_id`, `base_plan_id`,
  `purchase_token_hash text unique`, `expires_at timestamptz`, `verified_at`, `updated_at`; RLS **select own**,
  **no write grants** to `anon`/`authenticated` (writes come from the Vercel endpoint with the service-role key, which
  lives only in Vercel env — never in the APK, consistent with CLAUDE.md).
- `lucento_private.max_partners(owner)` → `case plan when 'PRO' then 1 else 0 end` from `entitlements` (default 0),
  with the existing procedural enforcement untouched. A `tools/sql-verify` suite case per plan.
- `consume_ai_quota()` keeps counting; the proxy adds a plan lookup (one `select` under the user's JWT, RLS-safe) and
  applies `AI_FREE_MONTHLY_LIMIT` / `AI_PRO_DAILY_LIMIT`; Smarter reading answers `403 pro_required` for Free.
- `lucento-website/src/pages/api/billing/verify.ts`: receives `{purchaseToken, productId}` with the user's Supabase JWT,
  calls the **Google Play Developer API** (`purchases.subscriptionsv2.get` / `purchases.products.get`) with a
  service-account key held in Vercel env, upserts `entitlements`, returns the state. Linking rule: one purchase token
  links to **one** Supabase user at a time; a re-link to another account moves it (latest verified wins) and the
  previous account drops to Free on its next refresh.
- `api/billing/rtdn.ts`: Pub/Sub push endpoint for Real-Time Developer Notifications (renewed, cancelled, in grace,
  on hold, revoked, expired, paused) → re-verify and update the row. The app learns the change on next refresh or
  through the sync cycle's existing membership/entitlement check.
- Not signed in: the app still works from Play alone (§6.3); server verification is best-effort and happens at the
  next sign-in. Server-paid features (sync, Shared, AI) all require sign-in anyway, so they are always
  server-verified before they cost anything.

### 6.5 Grants (D-9)

`source = GRANT` rows written by the owner (SQL, or later a tiny admin script) give founders and testers Pro with an
expiry. The app renders "Lucento Pro · courtesy access until …". No Play purchase, no code path differences beyond
the source label.

### 6.6 Debug, CI and tests

- `FakePurchaseSource` in debug and CI builds (the applicationId `.debug` can never bill). A **test-build-only**
  section in Settings › Lucento Pro ("Simulate: Free / Pro / Expired / Pending / Billing unavailable"), the same
  convention as the existing test-build "Switch account" (feature/lucento-shared).
- JVM tests drive every state through the fake; no Play dependency in `app/src/test`.
- `ScreenshotTourTest` gains Pro-screen and locked-sheet frames.

### 6.7 Database implications

**No Room migration is needed.** Usage counts are live queries (`GoalDao.countActive`), entitlement state is
DataStore, server state is one new Supabase table (append-only migration, RLS, grants to `authenticated` select
only). Backup format unchanged.

## 7. Risks, data integrity and backward compatibility

### 7.1 Rules (apply to every limited feature)

- Introducing or lowering a limit never deletes, hides, archives, or stops editing anything that exists.
- Counts are of **active** items; the user can always reduce usage themselves (archive a goal).
- A Free user over a limit sees the limit sheet once per attempt, with the count and the way out.
- Locked features keep their **data paths** alive: foreign wallets keep quoting, Shared members keep syncing,
  AI consent stays stored; only *new* entry is gated.

### 7.2 Sync lapse

Uploads and pulls pause; the chip says why; dirty rows wait (the queue already survives restarts); nothing on the
server is deleted; renewing resumes within the next cycle. A lapsed user can still export a JSON backup (which
includes unpushed tombstones — docs/BACKUP.md). Risk: a lapsed user edits on two phones for weeks, then renews —
the existing conflict rule (revision + causal, losing side notified) handles it; worth one explicit test.

### 7.3 Shared Space lapse (D-5)

Recommended at launch — **lenient**: `max_partners` → 0 only prevents *new* invitations/acceptances (that is
literally what the RPCs check today). An existing Partner stays, both keep syncing. Zero data risk, zero new server
logic; small revenue leak (one Owner can keep sharing after cancelling). Stricter options for later, in order of
cost: (a) Partner's writes refused after a 30-day grace via a policy clause reading `entitlements`; (b) membership
auto-closed with the Partner's phone keeping a read-only local copy. Both need RLS work and a Partner-side UX; do not
build before the lenient version has data.

### 7.4 Account and identity edge cases

- **Google Play account ≠ Lucento account.** Pro follows the Play account on the device. Signing out of Lucento's
  sync account keeps Pro locally; the server link moves to whichever account verifies next (§6.4). Document this in
  the Pro page ("Pro is tied to the Google account you bought it with in Google Play").
- **Shared Partner**: reads the Owner's plan from the space summary; the Partner's own plan is irrelevant for shared
  features but applies to their own-ledger features if they leave.
- **Device clock manipulation / rooted devices**: accepted risk for offline grace; the server is authoritative for
  everything that costs money.
- **Restore on a new phone** for a lapsed Pro: JSON backup remains; the account restore path offers the trial/plans.

### 7.5 Release and platform risks

- **No release signing yet** (CS-20) — a blocker for Play testing. Create the upload key and add the
  `LUCENTO_KEYSTORE_*` secrets first.
- **Target API**: confirm whether Play now requires `targetSdk 36` for new submissions (one source claims so; §4.5).
  If yes, the AGP/compileSdk upgrade (and Lottie 6.6.8+) lands before Phase 6.
- **Library churn**: Billing 8 was a breaking major, 9 shipped May 2026; stay on the latest 9.x and keep the Play
  code behind `PurchaseSource` so a future major touches one file.
- **Pending purchases** are common in PH (cash at convenience stores); the UI must survive a purchase that completes
  hours later while the app is closed (handled by `queryPurchasesAsync` on start + RTDN).
- **Policy**: no selling outside Play, clear trial/renewal disclosure, acknowledge within 3 days, restore available.
- **Website / app drift**: §8.
- **Existing testers' synced data**: the owner's and testers' accounts are already syncing on production; grant them
  Pro (§6.5) *before* the gated build reaches them so nothing pauses unexpectedly.
