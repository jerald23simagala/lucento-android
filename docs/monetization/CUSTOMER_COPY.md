## B. The Lifetime ownership promise (website `/lifetime`, in-app "What Lifetime means")

### Pay once. Own your budgeting experience for life.

**What "Lifetime" means.** You pay once, and every Pro feature that runs on your phone is yours for as long as Lucento
exists as an app: no renewal, no expiry, no subscription to keep alive. Google Play records the purchase on your Google
account, and Lucento remembers it on your phone, so it keeps working whether or not you are online.

**What is permanently included.** Everything in the "Pro Lifetime" column of the comparison that does not need
Lucento's servers:

- Unlimited money wallets, unlimited cards and loans, receivables, and things you own at a value you set.
- Wallets in other currencies, with exchange gains and losses worked out for you.
- Up to 20 active savings goals.
- Insights over 3, 6 or 12 months.
- Statements as PDF.
- All five themes.
- Every Pro feature we add later that runs on the phone. If a future feature needs servers, it is an online service,
  and we will say so.

**What works offline.** All of the above, plus everything Free already does: recording, history, budgets, debts,
schedules, Ask Lucento's on-phone answers, backup files, CSV. Your financial data is stored in a database on your
phone. Lucento does not check your purchase every time you open it, and it never needs to.

**What is an online service, and what you get.** Cloud sync, restoring from your account on a new phone, the AI
adviser's Pro allowance, Smarter reading and Lucento Shared run on servers that cost money every month. Lifetime
includes them for **12 months** from your purchase. After that you can keep them with **Lucento Cloud**
(₱59 a month or ₱490 a year), or carry on without them: your data stays on your phone, backup files and CSV keep
working, and the AI adviser falls back to the free allowance of 10 questions a month. Nothing you own is affected.

**If Lucento's servers are ever unavailable.** Everything you own keeps working, because none of it depends on our
servers. Only the online services would be affected, for everyone, for as long as the outage lasts.

**If you change phones.** Install Lucento and sign in to Google Play with the same Google account: your Lifetime
purchase is restored automatically, even before you sign in to Lucento. If you use a different Google account, sign
in to the Lucento account you used before and your purchase is restored from there. Your financial data is separate
from your purchase: bring it across with a backup file (free) or, while Lucento Cloud is active, restore it from your
account.

**If you uninstall the app.** Your purchase is safe with Google Play and with your Lucento account. Your data on that
phone is removed with the app, as with any app, so keep a backup file or cloud sync before you uninstall.

**Are AI and Cloud Sync included?** For the first 12 months, yes, at the Pro allowance. After that they are optional
with Lucento Cloud. We will never charge you automatically for Lucento Cloud; it is a separate choice you make, and
you can cancel it in Google Play at any time.

**Can I use Lucento for life without ever subscribing to anything?** Yes. That is the point of Lifetime.

## E. Customer FAQ

**1. Does Lucento work offline?**
Yes, completely. Recording, budgets, wallets, debts, goals, schedules, Insights and Ask Lucento's on-phone answers all
run on your phone with no internet and no account. Only sign-in, the AI adviser, cloud sync and Lucento Shared need a
connection, because they run on servers.

**2. Where is my data stored?**
In a database on your phone. If you turn on cloud sync, a copy is kept in your Lucento account on our servers so your
other phone can see it. Nothing leaves your phone until you sign in and turn sync on, or ask the AI adviser a question.

**3. What is Pro Lifetime, exactly?**
A one-time purchase of every Pro feature that runs on your phone, forever: unlimited wallets, cards and loans,
receivables, assets at a value you set, other currencies, 20 goals, 12-month Insights, PDF statements, all themes, and
future on-phone Pro features. It also includes 12 months of the online services. Read the full promise on the
Lifetime page.

**4. Is Lifetime the same as a subscription that never ends?**
No. A subscription rents all of Lucento, servers included, month by month. Lifetime is ownership of the part that lives
on your phone, which is why it can keep working offline and without our servers. The server part is a service, included
for a year and optional after that.

**5. Why isn't cloud sync included in Lifetime forever?**
Servers cost money every month, and a one-time price cannot honestly pay for them forever. Rather than promise it and
quietly degrade it later, we include a year and then offer Lucento Cloud at a low price. Everything you own is
unaffected either way.

**6. What happens to my Lifetime if Lucento's servers go down, or Lucento disappears?**
Everything you own keeps working, because none of it talks to our servers. Only sync, Shared and the AI adviser would
stop, for everyone, for as long as the outage lasts. Your data and backup files are always on your phone.

**7. I'm changing phones. How do I get my purchase and my data back?**
Purchase: install Lucento and use the same Google account in Google Play, and it is restored automatically. With a
different Google account, sign in to your Lucento account and it is restored from there. Data: carry a backup file
across (free), or restore from your account while cloud sync or Lucento Cloud is active.

**8. I uninstalled Lucento. Is my purchase gone?**
No. Google Play and your Lucento account both remember it. Your data on that phone is removed with the app, so make a
backup file or sync first.

**9. Does Lucento check my purchase every time I open it?**
No. Your phone remembers a verified purchase and Lucento trusts it. It checks with Google Play when it can, to pick up
changes like a renewal, but failing to check never takes anything away from you.

**10. Can I try Pro for free?**
Yes. Pro Annual has a 7-day free trial, once per Google account. Cancel in Google Play before the trial ends and you
are not charged.

**11. How do I cancel?**
In Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or from the Manage subscription link on Lucento's Pro page.
You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Lifetime has nothing to cancel.

**12. What happens to my data if I stop paying for Pro?**
Nothing is deleted or hidden. Everything you recorded, including wallets and goals beyond the free limits, stays and
stays editable. The free limits only apply to adding new ones. Cloud sync pauses and resumes if you come back; your
copy on the server is kept.

**13. Can I get a refund?**
Purchases are made through Google Play, which handles refunds under its own policy; you can request one from your
Google Play order history. If Google refunds a purchase, the matching plan ends on your account. Your data is never
affected by a refund.

**14. I'm a Pro subscriber and I want Lifetime. Do I pay twice?**
Buy Lifetime, then cancel the subscription in Google Play; you keep Pro until the paid period ends and Lifetime
from the moment you buy it. Google Play cannot credit the unused months automatically; if you have several months left,
contact us and we will extend your included online services by the same amount.

**15. How many AI questions do I get, and why is there a limit?**
Free: 10 a month. Pro, and Lifetime in its first year or with Lucento Cloud: 50 a day. Each question is answered by
Google's Gemini and costs us money, so there are limits; they are enforced on our servers and shown in the app. The
on-phone Ask Lucento statements have no limit.

**16. What does the AI adviser see?**
Your figures, your categories and your last 60 days of transactions for the question you ask, after you agree in the
app. Never your name, email, account numbers or notes. You can turn it off any time; everything else in Lucento works
without it.

**17. What is Lucento Shared, and does my Partner need to pay?**
Shared lets one other person, your Partner, see and record in your ledger from their own phone, with wallets labelled
Shared or Personal. The Owner's plan carries the Shared Space; the Partner needs no plan of their own. If the Owner's
online services end, nobody is removed and the Partner keeps working; the Owner just can't send new invitations.

**18. Why is there a wallet limit on Free?**
Because wallets are where Lucento's depth is: logos, cycles, reminders, currencies, net worth. The free limit
(3 money wallets, 2 cards or loans) is enough to run a real budget, and everything you add before upgrading, or
had before downgrading, stays yours.

**19. Will prices change?**
They may, and we will say so before they do. A Lifetime you bought stays yours at the price you paid. Subscription
price changes follow Google Play's rules, which notify you and ask for your consent where required.

**20. Can I move my purchase to a different Lucento account?**
A purchase belongs to the Google account that bought it and to the first Lucento account that used it. You can recover
it on any phone with either of those. It cannot be handed to an unrelated account; that protects you against someone
else claiming your purchase.

**21. Do you keep my data if I delete my account?**
Deleting your Lucento account removes your data from our servers. Your phone's copy stays until you remove the app or
clear its data, and backup files you saved are yours.
