# Regional prices on Google Play

Owner decision 2026-11-21: **four income tiers**, applied through the Play Developer API by `tools/play-pricing/`.
**Applied the same day:** all 870 prices (5 prices × 174 regions) were read back from Play and match the plan.
**Lifetime lowered later that day** (owner, to stay competitive with the closest apps): ₱2,490 / $59.99. Only the
Lifetime price changed (174 regions), and all 870 prices were verified again.
This replaces Play's straight exchange-rate conversion of the peso prices (MONETIZATION.md §4). The app needs no
change, because it only ever shows Play's own price strings (`ProOffers`, `ProCopy.yearlySavingPercent`). The
per-region table Play actually holds is [REGIONAL_PRICES.md](REGIONAL_PRICES.md), generated by the tool.

## Why

Until now each price was set in pesos and Play converted it for the other 173 regions. Read from Play on 2026-11-21
(`tools/play-pricing/history/2026-11-21-before.json`):

| Region | Pro monthly | Pro yearly | Lifetime | Cloud monthly / yearly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Philippines (set) | ₱149 | ₱1,190 | ₱3,490 | ₱59 / ₱490 |
| United States (converted) | $2.39 | $18.99 | $54.99 | $0.89 / $7.99 |
| Germany (converted) | €2.49 | €19.99 | €59.99 | €0.99 / €8.49 |
| India (converted) | ₹260 | ₹2,100 | ₹6,100 | ₹100 / ₹850 |

In the richest markets that is 30–40 % below the USD plan in MONETIZATION.md ($3.99 / $29.99 / $79.99 / $1.49 /
$12.99). And there was no discount at all where incomes are far below the Philippines'. An exchange rate is not a
price.

## The tiers

Prices are what a buyer pays. Play adds local tax where store prices include it (the Philippines, most of Europe, the
UK, Japan, Australia, India and others); US prices exclude sales tax. Tier bases are in `tools/play-pricing/tiers.json`.

| Tier | Base (converted by Play) | Pro monthly | Pro yearly | Lifetime | Cloud monthly | Cloud yearly | Regions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Full price | USD, before tax | $3.99 | $29.99 | $59.99 | $1.49 | $12.99 | 38 |
| 2 About 75 % | USD, before tax | $2.99 | $22.99 | $47.99 | $1.19 | $9.99 | 27 |
| 3 Philippine level (about 60 %) | PHP, VAT included | ₱149 | ₱1,190 | ₱2,490 | ₱59 | ₱490 | 52 |
| 4 About 40 % | USD, before tax | $1.59 | $11.99 | $23.99 | $0.59 | $4.99 | 57 |

- **The Philippines is pinned** to the approved peso prices, never converted.
- **Euro countries pay one euro price per tier**, VAT included (owner decision 2026-11-21). Tier 1: €3.99 / €29.99 /
  €59.99 / €1.49 / €12.99. Tier 2 (Portugal, Greece, the Baltics, Slovakia, Slovenia, Croatia): €2.99 / €22.99 / €47.99 /
  €1.19 / €9.99. Play's own conversion would have charged €3.59 (San Marino) to €4.59 (Finland) for the same tier,
  because VAT differs. Bulgaria (tier 3) and the West African countries Play prices in euros (tier 4) use their tier's
  conversion. In `tiers.json`: `currencyPrices`.
- **Two countries are hand-set** where Play's rounding went wrong (`overrides`, each with its reason). In Paraguay, a
  year cost more than twelve months (10,000 a month, 125,000 a year), so monthly is now 15,000 and Cloud 6,000. In
  Kenya, rounding to KES 200 left yearly only 25 % cheaper, so monthly is now 250 and Cloud 99.
- **Tier 3 is the Philippine price** converted by Play at today's rate. That is close to what those regions had
  already, and each run of the tool corrects any exchange-rate drift.
- Yearly Pro is 29–43 % below twelve months everywhere (mostly 30–37 %), and Lifetime is 2.6–3.0 × yearly. Cloud
  yearly is designed at about 27 % below twelve months; Play's rounding puts it at 15–24 % in a few regions (Serbia,
  Myanmar, Canada, Switzerland, Singapore and the West African euro countries). The app's "save X %" badge is worked
  out from each country's own amounts, so it is always true where it's shown.
- Tiers follow income and price level. Tier 1 is high income with high prices. Tier 2 is high income with lower
  prices. Tier 3 is upper-middle income, plus the lower-middle-income countries closest to the Philippines (itself
  lower-middle income). Tier 4 is the rest of lower-middle and low income. It's a judgement, recorded here so it can
  be challenged: move a country by changing its number in `tiers.json`.

## What changes for people

- **New purchases** use the new price. **Existing subscribers keep the price they joined at.** Play applies base-plan
  prices to new purchases only, and the tool never migrates price cohorts. Before launch, that only affects testers.
- The 7-day free trial on yearly Pro is a free phase, not a price, so it is unchanged everywhere.
- Play may take a few hours to show the new prices on every phone. The app reads them from Play at each launch and
  Restore.

## Running the tool

Needs Node 18+ and a service-account key whose Play Console permissions include **Manage store presence** (convert
and apply). The purchase-check account `lucento-play-billing` (docs/ACCOUNTS.md) gets it only while prices are
being changed. The key is read from disk and never printed or committed.

```
export LUCENTO_PLAY_KEY=/path/to/key.json
node tools/play-pricing/pricing.mjs plan                                     # read-only: out/plan.json + out/plan.md
node tools/play-pricing/pricing.mjs apply --plan tools/play-pricing/out/plan.json --yes
node tools/play-pricing/pricing.mjs verify --plan tools/play-pricing/out/plan.json
```

`plan` and `apply` save a snapshot of Play's current prices first. A snapshot is a rollback: `apply` it the same
way. After an apply, copy `out/plan.md` over `REGIONAL_PRICES.md` and commit it, so the repository records what Play
holds.

**Keep it current:** run `plan` every six months, and whenever a currency moves more than about 10 % against the
dollar (the Turkish lira, Argentine and Egyptian pounds and Nigerian naira move fastest). `plan` shows the drift as
now → new for each price.

**Don't use Play Console's price tools on these five prices.** Converting from a default price, or refreshing
exchange rates, overwrites the tiers. Change `tiers.json` and run the tool instead.

History: `tools/play-pricing/history/` keeps what Play held before the change (`2026-11-21-before.json`), what was
applied first (`2026-11-21-applied.json`) and the Lifetime change (`2026-11-21-lifetime-applied.json`). Any of them
can be re-applied.
