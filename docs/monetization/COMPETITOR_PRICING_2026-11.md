# Competitor pricing, 21 November 2026

How Lucento's prices compare with budgeting apps that do similar things. Prices were read on 2026-11-21 from each
vendor's pricing page, help centre or store listing (sources at the end). Spendee, YNAB and Monarch were read a second
time to check them. Prices change often, so check again before quoting any of them publicly. Lucento's own prices are
in [REGIONAL_PRICING.md](REGIONAL_PRICING.md).

## Verdict

- **The Pro subscription is competitive.** At $29.99 a year ($2.50 a month), 4 of the 17 yearly plans checked on
  Android are cheaper and 13 cost more. None of the four cheaper plans lists an AI adviser, and only Spendee Plus lists
  sharing. Plans with bank connections cost 1.3–6.7 × as much ($39.99–$199.99 a year).
- **Lucento is the cheapest confirmed way to get AI over your own figures.** Emma's Claude connector starts at
  $41.99 a year, Monarch's assistant comes with $99.99 a year, and Rocket Money's agent with its $15-a-month tier.
  Wallet's connector is the one exception we couldn't price, because its tier isn't stated. Lucento Pro includes 50
  adviser questions a day, and Free includes 10 a month.
- **In the Philippines** the subscription sits in the middle of the pack. ₱149 a month is ₱30 below Spendee Plus and
  ₱4 above Money Lover's Budget Plus. ₱1,190 a year is exactly Spendee Plus's yearly price.
- **Lifetime is the exception.** At $79.99 it's 2.7–4 × the lifetime price of Cashew, Money Lover and Wallet
  ($19.99–$29.99). At ₱3,490 it's 2–2.7 × theirs in the Philippines (₱1,290–₱1,790). It does include 12 months of
  sync, AI and Shared, and Spendee's Premium lifetime ($119.99 on the App Store) costs more. It's the one Lucento price
  that isn't clearly competitive at first sight.
- **What Lucento doesn't have:** bank connections. By design (privacy, offline, no paid APIs), Lucento relies on fast
  typed, spoken and scanned entry instead. The bank-sync apps charge for connections, and most only offer them in the
  US, Canada or Europe.

## Caveats

- **Not verified:** the Android prices of Bluecoins, Money Lover, Wallet, Monefy, 1Money and Fortune City; Wallet's
  plan periods; which of Spendee's lifetime items is current; Rocket Money's yearly price; PocketGuard's lifetime price
  (its own page refused automated reading). 1Money publishes no prices at all.
- Store ranges ("$x–$y per item") span every product an app sells, so they aren't plan prices.
- A price in a store doesn't mean the service works in that country. For example, Monarch, EveryDollar and Simplifi
  support US and Canadian banks only.
- MoneyWiz ($29.99–$59.99 a year) and Buddy ($9.99 / $49.99) are Apple-only, so they're left out of the comparison.
